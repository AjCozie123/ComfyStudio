Comfy Studio

How to add a workflow from ComfyUI
  1. Get the workflow file. Any ComfyUI workflow saved as a .json file works: one you downloaded, one from the ComfyUI template gallery, or your own (in ComfyUI: Workflow → Save or Export). Grouped nodes (subgraphs) are fine; the app opens them for you. If you set the workflow up in ComfyUI's App Mode, the app uses the controls you chose there.
  2. Upload it. On this page, tap + Upload workflow (top right) and choose the file.
  3. Wait a moment. The app opens the workflow the same way ComfyUI does and sets it up for you: your prompt, pictures, size, seed and so on go into the app's own buttons. It tells you in one line what the workflow makes. Most workflows say ✓ Ready to use.
  4. If it needs something, it asks one plain question (for example which text box is the prompt) or shows an Install button for an add-on your ComfyUI doesn't have yet.
  5. A file with several workflows in it (one for text to video, one for image to video ...) shows a list. Each one becomes its own choice; untick any you don't want.
  6. Tap Add. The app offers to use it straight away. It also appears on this page, marked Yours.
  7. Use it. Tap Use on its card. You go to Create with it selected; type a prompt and press the send arrow. The pills below the prompt (Size, Seed, LoRA and so on) now control this workflow.
  8. Switch back any time with the sliders button next to the send arrow (Settings → Engine), or tap Use on another card here. Delete on a card removes only that uploaded engine.

Settings the app doesn't control keep the values saved in the workflow. Built-in prompt enhancers inside a workflow are switched off, because the app's own Enhance pill does that job.

Quick start

  1. Pick Image or Video at the bottom left of the prompt box.
  2. Add pictures with + to edit one or use them as references. No pictures = a brand new picture.
  3. Type what you want in plain words, then check the pills above the box. The Auto settings are fine to start with.
  4. Send with the arrow. The result opens on the stage; tap Edit this to keep working on it.

The pills

A pill only shows when the engine you're using supports it, so the row changes between Create, Edit and Video.

MiniMax H3 Auto

Engine. The model and workflow that makes the picture. Auto picks the right one for what you're doing (new picture, edit, video).

Switch to Manual to force a model, e.g. Krea 2 for a different look. Add more engines on the Workflows page.

1:1 4 MP

Size. Shape and megapixels. More MP = more detail, but slower.

When editing, pick Match Picture 1 › Original for removals and small fixes so only what you asked changes. Then Upscale if you need it bigger.

Mode Edit Picture 1

Mode. Edit Picture 1 changes Picture 1 and keeps the rest. Reference makes a new scene and treats every picture as a reference.

Reference: a character in a new place, or a character sheet. Edit: changes to one photo.

Enhance Off

Prompt Enhancer. Rewrites your prompt with more detail before sending. Fast is light; Quality is slower and richer.

Good for short prompts on new pictures. Keep it Off for exact edits, where your own words matter most.

Camera Any

Camera. Angle, shot size, lens, focus and framing; one choice per group.

In Edit it re-shoots Picture 1 from the new viewpoint. In Video it adds motion: push-in, pan, tilt, orbit, tracking, handheld.

Roles Auto

Picture roles. Says what each picture is for: base, person, outfit, background or object.

Auto is usually right. Set it yourself when the model mixes up who or what comes from which picture.

Inpaint Paint

Inpaint. Paint the area that may change. Everything outside it stays exactly as it was.

Fix hands, swap an object, or place someone from Picture 2. Paint a little bigger than the thing so it fits whole, head and feet included.

Mark Area

Mark. Outline areas you can name in the prompt ("area 1") without limiting what changes.

Point at one of several similar things: "make area 1 red".

LoRA None

LoRA. Add-ons for a style, character or concept. Auto picks matching ones from your LoRA folder by reading your prompt, and adds their trigger words.

Strength 0.6 to 1.0 is a good range; up to 6 at once. Tap a LoRA to see or fix its trigger words.

Seed Random

Seed. Random gives a new variation each time. A fixed number repeats the same result for the same prompt.

Like a result? Tap Use last image's seed, then change one word at a time to fine-tune it.

?

Help. Short tips for what is on screen right now.

The sliders button next to Clear holds the engine settings (Auto or Manual, steps, models).

Video

16:9 720p

Video size. Shape and resolution. Sizes your graphics card can't make show greyed out with the memory they need (e.g. 1080p, 16 GB+).

480p is quick for tests, 720p for keepers. For 1080p on a smaller card, make it at 720p and use Upscale on the finished video.

Length 5s

Length. Type any number of seconds. Longer videos are made of short clips joined with no visible cut.

Clip length left empty = Auto, what fits your PC. Set it shorter for more control per clip or to save memory.

Shots 1, 2, 3

Shots. Write Shot 1: … Shot 2: … in the prompt and each shot becomes its own clip, in order.

Give a shot its own length with Shot 2 (3s):. Up to 7 clips per video.

Task Image to video

Video task. Text to video (no picture), Image to video (your picture is the first frame), References (people or things to include) and Long.

Adding a picture in Video switches to Image to video by itself.

Recipes

Make a new picture
  1. Image, no pictures attached.
  2. Pick a Size (shape and MP).
  3. Short idea? Set Enhance to Fast.
  4. Send. Use Seed to re-roll or to keep a look.
Remove or fix one small thing
  1. Add the photo (it becomes Picture 1, Mode: Edit Picture 1).
  2. Size: Match Picture 1 › Original.
  3. Inpaint: paint over the thing.
  4. Prompt: "remove the cup" or "fix her hand".
Put a person from another photo into yours
  1. Picture 1 = the scene, Picture 2 = the person.
  2. Inpaint: paint where they should go, a bit larger than their whole body.
  3. Prompt: "The woman from <Picture 2> is sitting on the sofa in <Picture 1>".
  4. Cut off at the edge? Paint the area bigger and send again.
Change clothes using a clothing photo
  1. Picture 1 = the person, Picture 2 = the outfit.
  2. Roles: set Picture 2 to Outfit if Auto gets it wrong.
  3. Prompt: "She wears the dress from <Picture 2>".
Same character, new scene
  1. Add the character's picture(s).
  2. Mode: Reference.
  3. Describe the new scene and pose.
The same photo from another angle
  1. Add the photo, Mode: Edit Picture 1.
  2. Camera: pick an Angle or a Move (e.g. Orbit left 45°).
  3. The prompt can be short: "same scene".
Turn a picture into a video
  1. Switch to Video and add the picture (Image to video).
  2. Length 5 s at 720p.
  3. Camera: a move such as Move closer (slow push-in).
  4. Describe the motion: "she turns and smiles, hair moving in the wind".
A longer video that tells a story
  1. Video, then write Shot 1: … Shot 2: … Shot 3: ….
  2. Length fills in by itself, one clip per shot.
  3. Keep each shot to one clear action.
Use a LoRA for a style or character
  1. Tap LoRA, turn on Auto or add one by name.
  2. Strength around 0.8.
  3. Trigger words go in by themselves; you'll see them in the LoRA panel.

      My words

      Always add

      Keep clothing

      All picture engines. When your prompt doesn't mention clothes (turn her, sit her down, change the light), the app adds one short "keep their clothing the same" line, so the edit can't change or remove what they're wearing. If your prompt is about clothes (add a jacket, wear the dress from Picture 2, remove the hat), nothing is added and your words decide. Body swaps and putting someone into a scene are left alone too, since their clothes come from the other picture.

      Keep image quality

      All picture edits. Adds one line so the result looks like the same photograph: the same sharpness, grain, skin and fabric texture, colours and lighting, with only your change. If your prompt changes the light (sunset, relight) the light isn't locked; if it changes quality (sharpen, restore) quality isn't locked; style changes (anime, black and white) are left alone.
