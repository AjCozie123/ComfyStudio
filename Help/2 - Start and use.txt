COMFY STUDIO - STARTING AND FIRST STEPS
======================================


STARTING
--------
1. Start ComfyUI first (see "3 - ComfyUI.txt").
2. Open Comfy Studio: the "Comfy Studio" shortcut on your Desktop / Start menu,
   or double-click "Start Comfy Studio.bat".

It opens in its own window. (If Windows' WebView2 is missing it opens in your
web browser instead - same app, at http://127.0.0.1:8198 .)

The dot at the top right says whether ComfyUI is connected.


FIRST TIME: GET THE ENGINES
---------------------------
1. Click "Workflows" at the top.
2. Each engine (MiniMax H3, Krea 2, FLUX.2 Klein, MOSS, Qwen Image, LTX ...) shows:
   - whether it fits your PC (from your graphics card and memory),
   - which model files it still needs.
3. If a banner says node packs are missing, click "Install all", then restart ComfyUI.
4. Click "Download" on the engines you want. Files come from their official pages
   and go straight into the right ComfyUI folder. Nothing downloads unless you press it.


MAKING THINGS
-------------
- Image / Video at the bottom switches what you make.
- Create = a new picture from your words.  Edit = change a picture you add with "+".
- The pills above the prompt set size, engine, Enhance (rewrites your prompt in detail),
  camera, LoRAs and more.
- Everything you make is in "Library".


CLOSING
-------
Close the window. ComfyUI keeps running until you close it yourself.
