How to add a workflow from ComfyUI
  1. Get the workflow file. Any ComfyUI workflow saved as a .json file works: one you downloaded, one from the ComfyUI template gallery, or your own (in ComfyUI: Workflow → Save or Export). Grouped nodes (subgraphs) are fine; the app opens them for you. If you set the workflow up in ComfyUI's App Mode, the app uses the controls you chose there.
  2. Upload it. On this page, tap + Upload workflow (top right) and choose the file.
  3. Wait a moment. The app opens the workflow the same way ComfyUI does and sets it up for you: your prompt, pictures, size, seed and so on go into the app's own buttons. It tells you in one line what the workflow makes. Most workflows say ✓ Ready to use.
  4. If it needs something, it asks one plain question (for example which text box is the prompt) or shows an Install button for an add-on your ComfyUI doesn't have yet.
  5. A file with several workflows in it (one for text to video, one for image to video ...) shows a list. Each one becomes its own choice; untick any you don't want.
  6. Tap Add. The app offers to use it straight away. It also appears on this page, marked Yours.
  7. Use it. Tap Use on its card. You go to Create with it selected; type a prompt and press the send arrow. The pills below the prompt (Size, Seed, LoRA and so on) now control this workflow.
  8. Switch back any time with the sliders button next to the send arrow (Settings → Engine), or tap Use on another card here. Delete on a card removes only that uploaded engine.

Settings the app doesn't control keep the values saved in the workflow. Built-in prompt enhancers inside a workflow are switched off, because the app's own Enhance pill does that job.

Start here

Comfy Studio makes and edits pictures and videos on your own PC, using ComfyUI behind the scenes. You type what you want; the app does the rest.

  1. Check it's ready. Top right should say ComfyUI connected with a green dot. If it says offline, press Start next to it and wait a minute.
  2. New picture? Just type what you want in the box at the bottom and press Enter (or the round arrow).
  3. Change a photo? Press + at the bottom left, pick your photo, then type the change, e.g. "make her jacket red".
  4. Your result appears on screen. Press Edit this to keep changing it, or find it later in Library.

You don't need to touch any settings to start. Everything is set to Auto, which picks good settings for you.

The screen at a glance

Create · Library · Guide · Workflows

The tabs at the top. Create is where you make things. Library holds everything you've made. Guide is this page. Workflows is where you add or switch the AI models (engines).

↑

Send. Starts making it. You can send more while one is running; they wait in a queue.

  • Enter sends. Shift + Enter starts a new line (on a phone, use the send arrow).
  • Write in normal sentences: "a woman reading in a sunny café, 35mm photo". The more you describe, the closer it gets.
  • When you attach pictures, call them <Picture 1>, <Picture 2>… Type @ in the box to pick one from a list.
  • Will also send (a small chip under the box) shows notes the app adds for you. Tap it to read them.

Adding pictures

  1. Press + at the bottom left of the prompt box.
  2. Choose where the picture is:
    • Library: something you've already made here.
    • From my PC: browse your PC's drives and folders. Recent shows new files, Browse lets you open folders (Up goes back a folder), Search finds by name. Tick pictures, then press Add.
    • Upload: pick a file from the device you're on (handy on a phone).
  3. The pictures appear as small squares above your text. Picture 1 (the first) is the one being changed. Others are extra: a person, an outfit, a background…
  • Shortcuts: drag a picture from your PC onto the prompt box, or copy it and press Ctrl + V in the box.
  • Remove one: the small ✕ on its square. Clear removes them all.
  • Adding a picture switches to Edit by itself (or to Image to video in Video).

The pills (the row of buttons above the prompt box)

Each pill is one setting. Tap it to open its choices; tap outside to close. Only the pills that suit what you're doing are shown, so the row changes between Create, Edit and Video.

Seeing all the pills: the row is wider than the screen. On a PC: roll the mouse wheel while pointing at the row, or click and drag it sideways. On a phone: swipe the row left or right. A soft fade at the edge means there are more pills that way.

MiniMax H3 Auto

Engine. Which AI model makes it. Auto picks the right one for what you're doing.

Leave it on Auto. To choose yourself, open Settings (⚙) and pick a model.

1:1 4 MP

Size. The shape (square, tall, wide) and how detailed (MP = megapixels).

For edits, pick Match Picture 1 › Original to keep your photo's size. Big sizes on small photos are handled for you (made at the photo's size, then enlarged).

Mode Edit Picture 1

Mode (Edit only). Edit Picture 1 changes your photo. Reference makes a brand-new scene using your pictures as examples.

Edit = "change this photo". Reference = "same person, new place".

Enhance Off

Enhance. Rewrites your words into a fuller description first. Fast is quick, Quality is richer but slower.

Good for short ideas. Keep Off when your exact words matter.

Camera Any

Camera. Tap tiles for angle (from above, from below), how close, lens and focus. In Video it also has movement: pan, zoom, push-in, handheld.

One choice per group. Clear removes them.

Roles Auto

Roles (2+ pictures). What each picture is for: the main photo, a person, an outfit, a background, an object.

Auto usually works. Set it if the result mixes things up.

Inpaint Paint

Inpaint. Paint over the part to change; everything else stays exactly the same.

See Inpaint for the painting tools.

Mark Area

Mark. Draw a box to point at something, so your words can say "the area" without limiting what changes.

Drag a box, then Insert.

LoRA None

LoRA. Add-ons for a style, a character or a look. Auto-pick chooses matching ones from your prompt and adds their trigger words for you.

Type a name and press Add. Strength 0.6–1.0 is a good start. Tap a LoRA to see its trigger words.

Seed Random

Seed. Random gives a new version every time. A fixed number repeats the same result.

Love one? Use last image's seed, then change one word at a time.

Making videos

  1. Switch the prompt box to Video.
  2. Pick the video type with the Text ▾ button: from words, from a picture (your picture is the first frame), from references, or a long video. Adding a picture picks "from a picture" for you.
  3. Set size and Length in the pills, describe the movement, and send.
16:9 720p

Video size. Shape and sharpness. Greyed-out sizes (e.g. "1080p · 16 GB+") need a bigger graphics card.

480p for quick tests, 720p for keepers.

Length 5s

Length. Type the seconds and press Set. Long videos are joined from short clips with no visible cut.

Clip length: leave empty (Auto) unless you want control over each piece.

Shots

Shots. Write Shot 1: … Shot 2: … and each shot becomes its own clip, in order.

Upscale

Upscale (on a finished video) makes it bigger and sharper with what your PC can run.

Settings: Auto or Manual, and where things are

Open Settings with the ⚙ sliders button at the bottom right of the prompt box (left of the send arrow).

Model family / Workflow

Pick the engine. Choose a model family (e.g. MiniMax H3, Krea 2), then a workflow. Add more on the Workflows page.

Auto | Manual

Auto (recommended): steps and other technical values follow the model, and the helper lines are chosen for each request. Manual: you set Steps, CFG, Sampler and Scheduler yourself, and tick which helpers to use.

Keep clothing / Keep quality

Helpers. Keep the person's clothes unless you ask about clothes; keep the photo's look in edits. They step aside by themselves when your request changes that very thing.

Auto understanding

Adds clear notes about which picture is which (in Mode). Under Settings you can add your own words for it.

Prompt Enhancer instructions

For advanced users: what the Enhance pill tells the rewriter.

Models

Which model files the engine uses (only change these if you know you need to).

ComfyUI memory

Auto (recommended): if a picture or video runs out of graphics memory, Comfy Studio restarts ComfyUI in a memory-saving way and makes it again by itself. Manual: choose Normal, Low memory or Lowest memory yourself (slower, but fits bigger models on smaller cards).

Library

  • Filters: All / Created / Edited, All / Images / Videos, ★ Favourites, and one engine.
  • Open a picture: tap it. Use ‹ › (or swipe) for the next one. Buttons: Edit this, Inpaint, Compare, Copy prompt, Reuse settings, Favourite, Delete.
  • Pick several: press Select (or press and hold a picture). Tick the ones you want, then Select all, ★ Favourite, Show in folder or Delete. ✕ stops selecting.
  • Delete all deletes everything shown (it asks first). Deleted files go to the Recycle Bin, so you can get them back.
  • Open folder (on the PC) opens the folder where everything is saved.

Inpaint: painting what to change

  1. Attach your photo, then tap the Inpaint pill (or Inpaint on a result).
  2. Brush: paint over what should change. Box: drag a rectangle. Erase: take paint away. Size sets the brush size.
  3. Paint a little bigger than the thing, so whatever goes there fits whole (head and feet included).
  4. + Area starts a separate area; you can refer to "area 1", "area 2" in your words. ↶ Undo / ↷ Redo, Invert, Clear help fix mistakes.
  5. Press Done, type what should happen there, and send. Only the painted part changes.

Workflows (engines)

  • Each card is an engine: a model that makes pictures or videos. Use switches to it.
  • "Needs N files" means the model files aren't on your PC yet: Download fetches them from the official source (only when you tap it).
  • + Upload workflow adds your own ComfyUI workflow. The app works out where your prompt goes and can make a short Test run.
  • App section: updates, and the switch "Install important fixes automatically".

Updates

Comfy Studio checks for updates about a minute after you open it, then every 12 hours. A banner at the top tells you what's happening:

Important update · automatic

An important fix is ready. It waits until nothing is generating: let your picture finish, or press Stop and install now.

Installing in 15 s

A short countdown, then the app restarts by itself and the page reloads.

Important update

If you turned off automatic fixes: press Install now or Later.

Minor update

Install whenever it suits you: Install or Later.

Updated

Shown once after an update, with What's new.

Updates never touch your pictures, PIN or settings, and a failed update undoes itself.

Recipes: how to do common things

Make a new picture
  1. Image, no pictures attached.
  2. Pick a Size.
  3. Short idea? Set Enhance to Fast.
  4. Type and press Enter.
Remove or fix one small thing
  1. Press + and add your photo.
  2. Inpaint: paint over the thing.
  3. Type "remove the cup" or "fix her hand" and send.
Change clothes, colours or hair
  1. Add your photo.
  2. Type the change: "make her dress black", "give him short hair".
  3. Only that changes; the rest is kept.
Put a person from another photo into yours
  1. Picture 1 = the scene, Picture 2 = the person.
  2. Inpaint: paint where they go, a bit larger than their whole body.
  3. "The woman from <Picture 2> is sitting on the sofa in <Picture 1>".
Give someone the face and hair from another photo
  1. Picture 1 = the body/scene, Picture 2 = the face.
  2. "She has the face and hair of <Picture 2>".
  3. If her head isn't in Picture 1 yet ("reveal her face"), the app does it in two steps for a natural result.
Same person, new scene
  1. Add their picture(s). Mode: Reference.
  2. Describe the new place and pose.
See the photo from another angle
  1. Add the photo. Camera: pick an angle or a move.
  2. Type "same scene" and send.
Turn a picture into a video
  1. Switch to Video and add the picture.
  2. Length 5 s, 720p, a Camera move.
  3. Describe the motion: "she turns and smiles".
A longer video with scenes
  1. Video, then write Shot 1: … Shot 2: ….
  2. Length fills in by itself.
Use a LoRA for a style or character
  1. Tap LoRA; turn on Auto-pick or add one by name.
  2. Strength around 0.8.

When something goes wrong

ComfyUI offline

Press Start at the top right and wait a minute. If it stays red, restart ComfyUI yourself.

It changed too much

Use Inpaint to limit the change, or set Size to Match Picture 1 › Original.

It ignored my words

Say it more simply and specifically ("make the left cup red"). Turn Enhance off for exact edits.

Out of memory / very slow

With ComfyUI memory on Auto (Settings), Comfy Studio restarts ComfyUI in a memory-saving way and tries again by itself. Otherwise use a smaller Size or video resolution, or a shorter video.

Something looks stuck

Stop at the top cancels it. Console shows what's happening.

      My words

      Always add

      Keep clothing

      All picture engines. When your prompt doesn't mention clothes (turn her, sit her down, change the light), the app adds one short "keep their clothing the same" line, so the edit can't change or remove what they're wearing. If your prompt is about clothes (add a jacket, wear the dress from Picture 2, remove the hat), nothing is added and your words decide. Body swaps and putting someone into a scene are left alone too, since their clothes come from the other picture.

      Keep image quality

      All picture edits. Adds one line so the result looks like the same photograph: the same sharpness, grain, skin and fabric texture, colours and lighting, with only your change. If your prompt changes the light (sunset, relight) the light isn't locked; if it changes quality (sharpen, restore) quality isn't locked; style changes (anime, black and white) are left alone.
